## Claude.com

—

A site designed to think alongside you

Marketing

Web

Most AI brands lean toward speed. Anthropic stands for depth. For the launch of its “[Keep Thinking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FDNkDBNR7AM)” campaign and [Sonnet 4.5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-4-5), we reimagined [claude.com](https://claude.com/product/overview) to reflect that stance—centered, spacious, product-first.

Inspired by the research process itself, we introduced a system of marginalia: subtle, contextual cues that echo how ideas evolve. Not decoration, but dialogue. A framework for deeper exploration—giving Claude a clearer voice, and users a more thoughtful way in.

Special thanks to our friends at Anthropic, Cali Pitchel, Em Dubovoy, Leo Succar, Jordan Gilroy, Kyle Craven, Logan Sparlin

## Antimetal

—

A thoughtful foundation for a visionary cloud brand

Brand

Marketing

We were tasked with defining the strategic and visual foundation to announce Antimetal's next‑generation cloud compute platform. Our strategic and narrative work led us into an identity system built around the motif of the atom—represented by a dot, this building block of the universe is used to express automation, intelligence, and scale.

We paired this with the concept of aether: the invisible fifth element binding the physical and digital, echoing a belief in boundless infrastructure. From there, a sunlight-inspired color system and dimensional motion studies bring warmth and tactility to an otherwise invisible product. The result is a brand built to evolve with Antimetal as they grow.

Special thanks to our friends Cali Pitchel, Brittany Sterling, Brent Clouse, and Tameem Sankari

## Nike

—

An experimental visual treatment for the 2024 Paris Olympics.

Marketing

We created a series of dynamic visuals for Nike's "Art of Victory" exhibition at the Centre Pompidou in Paris. Using TouchDesigner, we developed custom programs that transformed imagery and video into intricate pixel tapestries, forming bold visuals of Nike products and athletes in motion.

These pieces celebrate victory in all its forms—on the grand stage, in daily life, and in every small triumph—capturing the essence of Nike and the 2024 Paris Olympics.

Special thanks to our friends at Porto Rocha for bringing us on board, to Nike for the opportunity to celebrate the art of victory, and to the brilliant minds behind TouchDesigner—one of our favorite tools.

## Render

—

A refreshed visual identity and web experience for the cloud infrastructure disruptors.

Brand

Web

Render reached out to us in the Fall of 2023 to take their growing brand to the next level, through a design system built with motion in mind.

Special thanks to our friends Brendon Avalos, Cali Pitchel and Logan Sparlin.

![Render mobile site](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/5jjj3zhb/production/74ccd0364e1219c72479e8d228f8f04f30964bf8-5000x5620.jpg?w=640&auto=format)

Every project, for clients, for friends, or just for ourselves, comes from the same experimental practice: one that pairs new technologies and methods with time-tested design principles. This practice takes on different shapes. Visual identities and design systems. Open source research or tools. Custom typefaces. Experimental websites and apps.

The output changes, project to project. What doesn't: we're building things that are useful and enjoyable, for our creative community, for our clients, and for the people they serve.

### Figma Buzz

For the launch of Figma Buzz, we designed hundreds of templates using a system we created from the ground up: a uniform color palette, flexible grid, and illustration guidelines that gave our team a shared foundation to work from.

### Monostitch.wiki

[Monostich.wiki](https://monostich.wiki) turns randomness into (a sort of) revelation (at times). An adjective, a noun, and a Wikipedia photo combine in ways that surprise and delight—proof that meaning can emerge from the unexpected. Code is open-sourced [here](https://github.com/design-business-company/monostich-wiki).

### QSL Cards

[QSL Cards](https://www.are.na/jordan-egstad/qsl-cards-r_wluzxoxdi/table) are more than just confirmations of radio contact—they’re snapshots of a global community. Gathered as part of our design research, this collection of 14,340 cards celebrates the creativity, history, and human connection behind amateur radio, shared here for others to explore.

### Pal(ette)

![A grid of colors generated using Pal(ette), showcasing a range of systematically crafted hues with uniform saturation and luminance. The palette highlights the tool's ability to create balanced and accessible color schemes.](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/5jjj3zhb/production/23ef44d3ab8a90ba7c790e09da28c42f7d085f5a-2048x2560.png?w=640&auto=format)

[Pal](https://pal-ette.netlify.app/)(ette) is our proprietary tool for creating perceptually uniform color palettes. With fine-tuned controls over hue, saturation, and luminance, it empowers designers to craft systematic, beautiful, and accessible palettes. A tool designed to make colors work—and shine—for as many eyes and abilities as possible.

### Kala.watch

[Kala](https://kala.watch), inspired by the Sanskrit word for 'time,' transforms time into a living, breathing visualization. Its dynamic animations and minimalist design turn moments into a precise, poetic experience—a celebration of the time we often overlook. Code is open-sourced [here](https://github.com/egstad/kala).

### Tulsa Sky

Every ten minutes, we capture real-time weather and lunar data an programmatically draw an image of the [Tulsa sky](https://www.are.na/jordan-egstad/tulsa-sky) (Jordan's hometown). These abstract images, drawn by a Node.js app, capture the shifting hues of day and night—moments of light, shadow, and color in constant motion.

### Peg

![An image displaying the word 'Peg' repeated three times, with each instance showcasing the variable font axes interpolating from 0 to 1000. The transitions move from sharp to smooth edges, with puckered details in between.](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/5jjj3zhb/production/d283019bc47b49ae7de03aec36527b34c781caf9-2048x2560.png?w=640&auto=format)

Peg is a variable font inspired by embroidered typefaces crafted by Jordan's grandmother. Named in her honor, its variable axes shift from sharp to smooth edges, with puckered transitions reminiscent of stitched embroidery. Available freely on request.

### DetectScroll.js

[DetectScroll.js](https://github.com/egstad-construct/detect-scroll) is a lightweight JavaScript library for tracking scroll activity with precision and ease. Used on thousands of sites (including this one), it simplifies scroll direction and position detection, making scroll handling MUCH easier.
