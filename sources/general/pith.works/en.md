## Explaining the practice

We build web experiences and digital products for agencies, brands & designers emphasising performance, accessibility and thoughtful design. Research and collaboration are inherent to our practice and we use technology as a vehicle to create culturally resonant work.

Founded by Toby Onono - a developer with ten years’ experience across leading agencies and global clients. Based in Berlin but working globally.

Outside the office, we love to chat all things on photography and videography - if you have an exciting idea or want to swap notes get in touch [here](mailto:info@pith.works).
