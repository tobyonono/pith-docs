## What We Do

Pith builds web experiences and digital products for agencies, brands, and designers. Our work spans the full development lifecycle — from a first discovery call through to launch, handover, and ongoing support. Below is a clear picture of where we operate and how we can help.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Frontend Development" icon="monitor" href="/dev/naming-conventions">
    High-performance, accessible interfaces built to last. We work in React and Next.js, use GSAP for animation, and approach every UI as a system — not a collection of one-off screens. Performance, accessibility, and visual fidelity are non-negotiable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Backend Development" icon="server" href="/dev/sanity">
    APIs, integrations, and CMS architecture that hold up under real-world use. We work with Sanity for structured content, WordPress and WooCommerce for content-managed and commerce sites, and build integrations that keep data flowing reliably between systems.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Integration & Systems Consulting" icon="cpu" href="/studio/technology-stack">
    Integrating AI capabilities into products and workflows — from LLM-powered features to automation pipelines. We help teams understand where AI genuinely adds value and how to implement it without overcomplicating the systems around it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Technical Consulting" icon="lightbulb" href="/studio/project-process">
    Code audits, architecture reviews, technology selection, and team mentoring. If you're inheriting a codebase, planning a rebuild, or need a second opinion before making a big technical decision — this is where we help.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Additional Services

Beyond core builds, we offer a range of focused engagements:

- **Code Audits** — A structured review of an existing codebase: performance, security, maintainability, and technical debt.
- **Bug Fixes** — Targeted fixes for existing sites and products, with clear documentation of what was found and resolved.
- **Platform Migration** — Moving from one CMS, framework, or hosting environment to another — with minimal disruption and no data loss.
- **Hosting & Maintenance** — Ongoing hosting management, dependency updates, uptime monitoring, and minor content changes.

## Capabilities

Across all of the above, Pith has direct experience delivering:

- **Informational Websites** — Clear, fast, purpose-built sites for organisations and individuals.

## Design Network

Development and design are most powerful when they work together from the start. Through Pith's network of trusted design collaborators, projects can access:

- **Brand strategy** — Positioning, messaging, and brand architecture.
- **Visual identity** — Logo, colour, typography, and brand systems.
- **Design systems** — Component libraries and pattern documentation built to scale with your product.
- **Art direction** — Creative direction for campaigns, photography, and digital content.

When a project needs it, we bring in the right collaborator and work as a joined-up team — so you're never managing two separate relationships.
