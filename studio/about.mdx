## Who We Are

Pith is a creative technology studio operating between Dublin and Berlin. We build web experiences and digital products for agencies, brands, and designers — work that is performant, accessible, and made with genuine care for the people who use it.

We are small by design. That's not a limitation — it's the whole point. Staying lean means every project gets real attention, senior judgment, and a direct line to the people actually doing the work. No layers. No handoffs to juniors after the pitch.

<Note>
  **Our core belief:** Technology is a vehicle, not a destination. The work that lasts is work that feels human — built with craft, grounded in culture, and made to serve real people in the real world.
</Note>

## The Studio's Origin

Pith was founded by Toby Onono, a developer with over ten years of experience across leading agencies and global clients. That time — working across scales, industries, and creative contexts — shaped a clear point of view: the best digital work happens when development is treated as a creative discipline, not a finishing step.

The studio takes its name from the word's dual meaning: the essential part of something, and the spongy core that gives a plant its structure. Both feel right. We try to get to the essential thing in every project, and we believe good structure is what lets creative work breathe.

## What We Believe

**Performance is craft.** A slow website is a broken website. We obsess over load times, rendering, and the feel of interactions — because these things are invisible when they're right and unforgivable when they're wrong.

**Accessibility is not optional.** Building for everyone isn't a compliance checkbox. It's a minimum standard of respect for the people using what we make.

**Research and collaboration are built in.** We don't parachute in with a pre-formed solution. Every engagement starts with genuine curiosity about the problem.

**Nimbleness beats scale.** We size project teams to what a project actually needs — and we've built a network of trusted design collaborators so we can flex when the work calls for it.

## Beyond the Screen

Outside of client work, we're drawn to photography and videography — practices that share a lot with development: attention to light, composition, timing, and the moment when technical knowledge gets out of the way of something true. That sensibility informs how we think about the web.

## How We Work

We work globally, pairing directly with agencies, in-house teams, and independent designers. Through our network of design collaborators, we can also bring in brand strategy, visual identity, and design systems capability when a project needs it — making Pith a fuller creative partner than a traditional development studio.

If you're reading this as a new collaborator: you've joined something intentional. We're glad you're here.
